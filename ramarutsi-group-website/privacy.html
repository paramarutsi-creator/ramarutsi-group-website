Privacy & POPIA notice

How this website handles information and what to know before using the enquiry form.

Website privacy notice · 1 November 2026

Ramarutsi Group (Pty) Ltd respects the privacy of people who visit this website. This notice describes the current behaviour of this site. It is not a substitute for a complete PAIA manual or a legal assessment of the Group's wider information-processing activities.

Information submitted through the enquiry form

The website enquiry form currently validates the information you enter in your browser and displays a message identifying your selected division. It does not send, store or retain your name, email address, division selection or message. Do not include confidential or sensitive personal information in the form.

Cookies, analytics and external services

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not include an analytics service. The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts and a background photograph from Unsplash. Those providers may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your device requests those resources. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy notices.

How to make an enquiry

The contact form is a front-end demonstration and is not connected to a message-delivery service. Submitting it will not contact the Group or a subsidiary. Use contact details independently verified through an official Ramarutsi Group channel to make a real enquiry or privacy request.

Privacy rights and this notice

South African data-protection law provides rights concerning personal information where it is processed, including rights to request access or correction and, where applicable, to object to processing. This website does not currently collect enquiry-form submissions. If a form-delivery service or other data-processing feature is added, this notice should be reviewed and updated before that feature is used.

Questions

No privacy contact address has been published on this website. Please use a contact channel independently verified as belonging to Ramarutsi Group before sharing personal information.
